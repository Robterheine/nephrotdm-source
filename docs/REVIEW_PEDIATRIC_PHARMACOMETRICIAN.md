## Required changes before release
1. (Should fix) Variance floor in `bayes.js makeOfv` (`Math.max(ADD + PROP*pred^2, 1e-6)`) is not in FOCE-I. It binds when predicted concentration < 2.1 ug/L (MPA) or < 5.2 ng/L (tacrolimus) and then moves the posterior: AUC median -11.8 / +21.6 / -5.4 %, trough -33 / +67 / -14 % in my three albumin-5 patients. Removing the floor in a scratch copy restores agreement (<= 1.9 % at q05/50/95). Either remove it for the pediatric specs or restrict albumin so such predictions cannot occur (see 2).
2. (Should fix, owner decision D9) Albumin lower limit 5 g/L: CL x118 (AUC 0.5 mg.h/L at 600 mg, 38.5 kg); at 10 g/L x21 (95 % CI of the exponent: x5.6 to x107). Data range 24-42. Recommend a visible stronger warning below ~15 g/L (CL x7.7) or a higher refusal limit.
3. (Should fix, wording) State that the MPA estimate treats the kappa of dosing days without a sample as exactly 0 (see Q3): a NONMEM stream with a free kappa for every dosing day changes the individual AUC by median 1.2 %, 90th pct 3.8 %, max 15 % (sparse designs).
4. (Documentation) Correct HANDOFF: the Vc IIV CI of Table 2 is 109.65-18 819.64 % (not 15-18 820 %); Table 2 CVs of Q, IOV and residual follow from the run-57 listing (0.339, 0.193, 0.224), not from the ESM values 0.337, 0.19, 0.223 that the code uses (see Q1).
5. (Minor) Tacrolimus haematocrit has no "outside data range" warning (D9 open item); data Ht ranges seen: 0.26-0.31 (Schijvens 2019, median 0.29), 0.29-0.45 (Heida 2026 evaluation).
6. (Minor) `mapBFGS` can end in a worse mode (see Q6).

  - Independent scipy matrix-exponential oracle (my own, python) on 150 random + 40 MPA / 600 tacrolimus pole-proximity cases (eta to +-5 SD, KA or KTR within 0, 1e-9, 1e-6, 1e-3, 3 %, 4.9 %, 5.1 %, 10 % of a pole, ss and histories): max rel. diff MPA 1.1e-11 / 2.3e-12, tacrolimus 1.0e-11 / 1.2e-12.   - Red-first: KTR 1.48->1.50 gives 8.7e-2; albumin reference 35 gives 1.5e-1; Vc 24.9->24.8 gives 5.4e-3 (all detected).
- Per-dose formulation vs NONMEM as published (record-wise KA; parameters at a record apply to the interval ending at that record, so the new KA acts retroactively on material in transit). Sweep: both directions, WT 10/25/60, dose spacing 6/12/24 h, typical eta, samples 0.25 h to trough after the first dose of the new formulation: app minus NONMEM-literal 1.1 to 3.5 % capsule->suspension (+), -0.3 to -3.5 % suspension->capsule; max 3.8 %, median 1.2 %; it persists to the trough because the in-transit prior dose is absorbed at the wrong rate in NONMEM. For a slow absorber (eta KA -2 SD, KA_cap 0.38/h) 13.8 % at 24 h post switch. No difference without a switch. Defensible: each dose is absorbed at its own formulation's rate (the physical case); NONMEM's behaviour is an artefact of one KA per record. F is per dose in both. Difference is well under the 19 % residual SD and confined to ~1-2 days after a switch.
- One-Ht approximation (PK path at the latest sample's Ht, each sample's own Ht for the blood reading) vs NONMEM record-wise Ht (48 histories, Ht drifting linearly over 7 days, 816 predictions): mean 3.5 %, median 0.8 %, p95 20 %, max 31 %. By |dHt|: 0.02 early (<=48 h) mean 1.4 %/max 2.9 %; 0.05: 3.5 %/7.3 %; 0.10: 6.6 %/11.9 %; 0.23: 17 %/31 %; late samples (>=168 h) <= 0.65 %. Effect on the reported (corrected) AUC after MAP, relative to the app's own constant-Ht world: lastOnly sampling 0.01-0.07 % mean; samples spread over a week mean 0.2-1.2 % (up to 10 % mean and 42 % worst in the 0.25->0.35 group, where MAP also hit the spurious basin, see Q6). Acceptable for samples taken at similar Ht (same day); the UI should say so when sample Ht values differ by > 0.05.

## Q3 Likelihood and MAP (G2)
- Likelihood: `ofv = eta' Omega^-1 eta + sum[(y-f)^2/v + ln v]`, v = sigma^2 f^2 is the FOCE-I individual objective for Y = IPRED(1+EPS) (interaction: v uses IPRED), kappas included as extra etas with the shared omega^2. Confirmed numerically.
- Own POSTHOC (METHOD=COND INTER MAXEVAL=0 POSTHOC), 24 patients per drug, designs not used by the builder (two samples at 6 and 8 h, trough only, odd times, 5 occasion days, extreme KA eta +-2.5 SD, WT 3/120, ALB 5/50, Ht 0.10/0.70, very low concentrations):
  - Tacrolimus: max |d eta| 4.5e-7, 24/24 within 1e-3, AUC difference <= 1.1e-5 %, objectives equal to 1e-11.
  - MPA with the app's convention (kappa only on sampled days, OCC 0 elsewhere): 21/24 within 1e-7. IDs 3, 7, 15 (ALB 5, observed 1e-4 to 1.3e-3 mg/L) differ (max |d eta| 0.29, AUC +34 %) and the app's objective is LOWER than NONMEM's: cause = variance floor 1e-6 (shown in L3 by removing it). Altering NONMEM sigma/omega gives 1/24 within 1e-3 (red-first).
  - MPA with a free kappa for every dosing day (6 occasions): max |d eta| 0.0035-0.07 typical, AUC differs median 1.2 %, p90 3.8 %, max 15 % (excluding the floor cases). NONMEM's unsampled-day kappas come out nonzero (to 0.19) because earlier doses carry over into the sample. Not an app error but a modelling convention (hand-off S4); which OCC coding the authors used for dosing days without a curve is unknown (author query).

## Q4 Posterior (L3)
Is medians within 5 % sufficient? No, on its own. It is lenient (Monte Carlo noise is about 1 %), blind to the 5/95 % limits that are reported, to P(in window) (the headline probability), and to shape (bimodal Vc). I compared 5/25/50/75/95 % quantiles of AUC and trough, P(in window) and the two-sample KS distance on my own 24 patients per drug (NONMEM BAYES BIONLY=1, 2000 burn-in, 6000 samples, one job at a time, population not all FIX; app: shipped `runFit`, default budget, 8 chains; exposures of NONMEM draws computed with the app engine, MPA AUC also from dose/CL independently, deviation < 1e-9).
- Tacrolimus: 24/24 within 5 % at every quantile; worst median 0.9 % (AUC), 0.7 % (trough); worst q05/q95 2.4 %/4.8 % (trough q95, ID 15); max |dP(in window)| 0.012; KS <= 0.036; all fits converged.
- MPA: 21/24 within 2.7 % (worst 2.7 % at q95); max |dP| 0.012 for those. IDs 3, 7, 15: KS 0.12-0.42 (see floor); with the floor removed in a scratch copy of `bayes.js`: median 0.8 / 0.0 / 0.8 %, KS <= 0.018. 
- Red-first: NONMEM BAYES with omega^2 CLINT 0.456 -> 0.200 gives only 15/24 within 5 % (AUC median worst 29 %, KS up to 0.52), so the comparison can fail.
- Sampler on the MPA Vc prior (WT 38.5, ALB 34): trough-only fit R-hat 1.000, min ESS 22 000, 1.1 s; Vc posterior stays prior-like (q05/50/95 = 1.1/13.8/168 L), AUC 56.1 [34.2, 96.8]; rich design Vc 1.4/10.9/33 L, shrinkage 0.39.

## Q5 Scientific soundness
(a) Steady-state AUC = dose/CL exactly: numerical Simpson integral of the engine over the interval, 60 random patients (WT 3-120, ALB 5-50, eta Vc sd 2, tau 8-14 h): max dev 3.7e-11. Independent of Vc, Vp, Q, KTR (they vary in these draws). Single-dose AUC(0-inf) = dose x exp(kappa)/CL to 1.6e-4 (quadrature limit). F effect enters as dose x exp(kappa) (same as F1 = exp(IOV) in NONMEM; verified against NONMEM in G1).
(b) Vc prior omega^2 2.42 (sd 1.56): 90 % prior interval at 70 kg 1.9-322 L; P(Vc<5 L) 15 %, P(>500 L) 2.7 %. Dangers: none for the AUC (independent of Vc); trough and early concentrations are prior-driven. Numerics hold to eta +-5 SD (Vc x0.0004 to x2400; engine vs scipy to 1e-11). Sampler converges (above). MAP can sit in the lower of two Vc modes (Q6), harmless for AUC.
(c) Tacrolimus weight scaling exactly as stream: QHP and CLINT x(WT/70)^0.75 (QHP also (1-Ht)), Q x(WT/70)^0.75, V3, V4 x WT/70, VL = 0.0437 WT^0.9 (unscaled by 70); confirmed by NONMEM at WT 3, 9, 120 kg.
(d) Extrapolation numbers. MPA albumin multiplier on CL: 24 g/L x2.38, 42 x0.59, 50 x0.38, 20 x3.7, 15 x7.7, 10 x21, 5 x118 (exponent CI -3.82/-1.41 gives at 10: x5.6-x107). Weight (limits 3-200 vs data 12.9-79.9 / 9.1-78): CL factor 0.094 at 3 kg, 2.2 at 200 kg; KTR x2.2 at 3 kg; VL 0.12 L at 3 kg; allometry without maturation is not supported below ~9 kg. Haematocrit 0.10-0.70 (data 0.26-0.45): same plasma curve, 25 kg, whole-blood AUC 68.7 (Ht 0.10), 143.8 (0.22), 187.8 (0.29), 225.7 (0.35), 289 (0.45), 448 (0.70); corrected 224-227 throughout (the intended behaviour); actual/corrected 0.31 to 1.97. QHP factor (1-Ht) 0.90 to 0.30. Statement needed: outside Ht 0.26-0.45 and weight/albumin ranges results are extrapolations; only weight and albumin warn today.
Other: the text "true single-day AUC scattered by a factor of about 1.2-1.3, on average 0-7 % above" (texts_mpaped) should be checked against the calibration record: unconditionally exp(kappa) has geometric SD 1.55 and mean +10 % (omega^2 0.19).
