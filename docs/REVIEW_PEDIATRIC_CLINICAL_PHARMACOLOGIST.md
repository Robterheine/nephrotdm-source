# Gate G6: clinical pharmacologist review of the pediatric MPA and pediatric tacrolimus texts (NephroTDM 1.5.0)

Seat: clinical pharmacologist (scope, safety, wording of windows and caveats). Written 5 October 2026 by an independent reviewer who did not write the code.
Scope of this review: the strings a user sees, the specs that feed them, and the shared screen/report wording as they apply to `mpaped` and `tacped`. Model numerics
(G1 to G4) are other seats' work and were not re-judged, except where a number in a text is checked against the engine.

**Method.** (1) Read `CLAUDE.md`, `HANDOFF_PEDIATRIC.md`, the two text files, the two specs, `ui.js`, `report.js`, `diagnostics.js`, `index.html`. (2) Rendered what a user sees with
node (Background, Getting started, About, spec strings, help, tiles, advisory notes, explorer output, A4 report for one fitted case per drug). Scripts are in
`/tmp/claude-501/review_cp/` (`dump.js`, `render.mjs`, `nums.js`; outputs `dump_*.txt`, `render_*.txt`). (3) Checked every number and claim against the three PDFs
(Heida 2024; Heida 2026 incl. Tables 1, 2, 4 to 7 viewed as images; Schijvens 2019) and the tacrolimus ESM (assay line). (4) Only after that, read
`CALIBRATION_RESULTS_PEDIATRIC.md`, `RELEASE_NOTES_V150.md` and `VERIFICATION_PEDIATRIC.md` to cross-check (section 7). Nothing in `src/`, `tests/`, `build.mjs`, `index.html`
or any existing file was changed. stdout and stderr of every run were read; no run printed an error.

## 0. Summary

- **No dose or interval advice was found** in any pediatric string. The wording "does not select or recommend a dose" is used consistently. Findings under question 2 are
  about two worked examples that pair a dose with a result, the label "Candidate maintenance dose", and an imperative ("Repeat measurements"). All low or medium.
- **Numbers.** All accuracy figures, both target tables and the three worked examples reproduce (examples re-computed with the engine: 194.1/225.4, 257.3/226.0 and MPA 58.7/36.2).
  The problems are not the numbers but (a) what they are compared with, (b) three claims stronger than the sources, and (c) one internal contradiction in the MPA wording.
- **Highest-severity findings**
  1. MPA: the screen note, the report scope line and the report "How to read" call the reported AUC "the day of the samples", while the reported AUC is the **typical day**
     (the engine sets occasion effects to zero). The scatter of 1.2 to 1.3 that the owner's own simulation found appears only in About. The 2026 accuracy figures
     (0.1 % / 21.0 %) are for the sampled-day AUC, not for the quantity the app prints.
  2. Tacrolimus: "**Defined for whole blood at haematocrit 0.35**" is stated as a fact for all six window sets (basis strings, Background, About, help, hint). The sources do not
     say that. Heida 2026 normalised its AUCs to 0.35 and compared them with the ranges; Schijvens 2019 *assumed* that literature targets refer to 0.35. For the troughs
     (Brunet 2019 via Heida 2026) nothing in the cited paper says it.
  3. The accuracy sentences ("estimated accurately", 0.1 %/21.0 %, 0.2 %/7.8 %) omit that the comparison is model against model (reference = AUC from the full profile
     estimated with the same model) in the children the model was built on, and that the app's own simulation did worse (MPA 26.3 %, tacrolimus 16.3 %).
  4. The ciclosporin exclusion for MPA is not on the screen at the point of use (only in Background and About). "Research use only" is not on the main screen at all (footer, About, report).
  5. Tacrolimus: the name of the chosen window set (the time since transplantation) is shown only in a 2.6 second toast; not on the tile, not in the report, not in the saved state.
     The hand-off (section 12) promised "set name on the tile and the report".
  6. Scope texts give weight ranges only. The data cover **ages 4 to 18 (MPA) and 1 to 17 (tacrolimus)**, mostly early after transplantation. There is no age field, so a toddler or
     infant is caught only by a weight warning that does not say "not covered".
- **Verdicts** (details in section 8): **MPA pediatric: pass with comments (conditional), not releasable as drafted. Tacrolimus pediatric: pass with comments (conditional), not
  releasable as drafted.** All required changes are wording or small label/state changes; none touches the model.

**Is showing "probability in window" for a candidate dose acceptable?** Yes, with conditions. It is the same contract as the adult drugs: one dose per press, no ranking,
no "best dose", no list sorted by probability, no suggestion in the text. But functionally it is a manual dose-finding aid: a user can press Explore repeatedly until the
"In the window" chip is high. The sentence "does not select or recommend" is true of the code and not a full description of the use. For children the evidence is that exposure
three months later could not be predicted, so the explorer note should say so next to the result (W19), and the card should call the number what it is (exposure under the model for the dose entered).
I would not remove it. If the owner wants a stricter reading of golden rule "never optimize", the cheaper alternative to removal is to hide the three probability chips in the explorer
and show only the median and interval; that is the owner's call, not a finding of unsafe wording.

## 5. Windows (question 5)

- **Labels.** Time since transplantation is in the AUC labels ("0-6 weeks after transplant"), but only partly ("AUC, 6 weeks-6 months", "Trough, first 2 months" lack "after transplant"). AUC versus trough is clear.
  Whole blood and haematocrit 0.35 are in the basis line under each set in the Background dialog, but not on the tile, the report or the toast; and the claim itself needs the correction above.
  The AUC sets change at 6 weeks and the trough sets at 2 months; they come from different sources (local guideline plus Wallemacq 2009 versus IATDMCT consensus) and do not line up. One sentence helps (W35).
  The local protocol in Schijvens 2019 Table 1 uses other trough bands (0-4 weeks 10-20, 4 weeks-6 months 5-15 or 7-12, 6-12 months 5-10, >12 months 4-8); a reader who knows that table will not find it here.
- **No set preselected.** This is the safe choice: any default would be wrong for most children (the correct set depends on a date the app never sees), and a silently wrong window gives confident
  probabilities. The empty window has its own risk: the tile then shows only "No AUC window is set, so no probabilities are shown." and a user may compare the number against remembered adult ranges.
  Mitigation without a default: make the empty-window sentence actionable (W24), and always print the chosen set name (W23).
- **Nephrotic syndrome as text.** Adequate as a decision (a one-sided window cannot be stored, and the model has no nephrotic data), but the current paragraph is incomplete and slightly mis-sourced.
  The Above-the-lower-bound chip already gives P(AUC > 50) whatever upper bound is typed, and the paragraph should say that and add the albumin caution (W8).
- **Side effect.** `applyWindowSet` clears the other window (AUC set clears the trough bounds and the reverse) without telling the user (W25).
