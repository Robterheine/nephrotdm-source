NephroTDM

Session
Model-informed therapeutic drug monitoring for kidney transplantation and nephrology. Choose a drug, enter the dosing and any measured concentrations, and the app estimates the patient’s exposure with its uncertainty and shows what a different dose would do. Everything runs locally in your browser: nothing you enter is sent or processed online. The only copy is an autosave in this browser.
New: everolimus for adult kidney transplant recipients (Zwart 2021) joins mycophenolic acid and tacrolimus, and the printed report is now one A4 page. Choose the drug in card 1. Each model’s parameters, scope and limits are in About.

1 Patient & covariates

Drug / PK model
Therapeutic window: AUC₀–12h – change
The target is the AUC over a 12-hour dosing interval at steady state (AUC₀–12h). Regimens with another interval are compared as their 12-hour equivalent: AUC₁₂ = AUC₀–ₓ × 12/ₓ.
Therapeutic window: trough – change
Both windows are clinical inputs from your protocol (see the background dialog for the IATDMCT consensus). Leave a window empty and the app reports intervals without probabilities.

2 Dosing schedule & measured concentrations

The app uses the exact periodic steady state of this regimen, ending with the latest dose. Time on the chart is in hours, with t = 0 h at the latest dose. To enter a dosing history that changed over time, switch to “Full schedule”.

Measured concentrations

Immediate-release tacrolimus, twice daily (Prograf-type capsules). Prolonged-release products (once daily) are not covered by this model.
EC-MPS: absorption is delayed and highly variable, the source model’s own conclusion is that sparse limited sampling is expected to be unreliable for EC-MPS. Prefer more samples spread across the dosing interval (e.g. 1.5, 2, 4, 6, 8 h), and treat trough-only results with particular caution.
